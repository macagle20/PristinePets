From the founder

The story behind Pristine Pets.

I earned my bachelor’s degree in Computer Engineering and served for six years as a military officer. I’d always been drawn to business, so I pursued a master’s in Business Analytics to get better at using data to make business decisions.

After I left the military, I started my first business: Pristine Pets. I chose mobile grooming because I love being around dogs and wanted to build a business that served my community. I liked the idea of making life a little easier for local dog owners by bringing grooming right to their door. I loved building the business and learned firsthand how finding customers, keeping the schedule full, and covering operating costs all fit together.

When my grooming van went out of commission, I had a decision to make: put another van on the road or put my time into other ventures. I chose to pursue those ventures and closed the grooming business.

I loved building Pristine Pets, being part of the grooming community, and learning what it takes to run a business of my own. I started Pristine Pets Consulting to stay connected to that community and share what I learned with people just getting started.
