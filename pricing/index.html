Good to know

Before you book.

Do I have to be a groomer?

No. The package is for people evaluating or starting a mobile grooming business. It does not teach hands-on grooming.

What will we work on during my call?

We’ll focus on the decision that matters most right now. If you’re weighing a launch, we might work out how many bookings you’d need to cover your costs. If you’re already taking bookings, we might look at whether your revenue can support hiring a groomer.

Does this include tax or legal advice?

No. The guide covers general planning and recordkeeping topics. For advice specific to your business, consult a qualified professional.
